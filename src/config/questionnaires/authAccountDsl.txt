# [c1] Purchase for others
BR
[c3] Add and manage verified recipients before buying gifts or other items for them.
BR
[c3] Each recipient should confirm their name, contact number, and mailing address before they can receive items. An account will be created for the recipient during that confirmation flow.
BR
[c3] You can keep up to 12 verified recipients on this list.
BR
[c3] Store purchases for other people will use this verified recipient list instead of rushed checkout entry fields.
