# [c2] Quick Rescue
GUIDETABLE|What you see|Most likely cause|What to do first||Wilting after planting|Transplant shock, hot sun, dry root ball|Shade briefly and water gently.||Few flowers|Too little sun or too much nitrogen|Give more sun and reduce leaf-growth feeding.||Leaves yellow|Overwatering, old leaves, poor drainage|Check drainage and remove old leaves.||Chewed leaves|Caterpillars, beetles, slugs|Inspect early and remove pests.||White powder|Powdery mildew|Improve airflow and avoid wet leaves overnight.||Flower heads fading|Normal aging|Deadhead to encourage more blooms.

# [c2] Care Basics
GUIDETABLE|Care area|Guide||Sun|Give full sun for strongest flowering.||Water|Water while establishing, then avoid keeping soil soggy.||Soil|Use well-drained soil. Compost helps, but do not overfeed.||Feeding|Too much nitrogen can make leaves instead of flowers.||Deadheading|Remove faded flowers for a cleaner plant and more blooms.||Containers|Use containers with drainage holes.

# [c2] Pests
# [c2] Diseases
GUIDETABLE|Problem|Signs|Action||Aphids|Sticky growth|Rinse and monitor.||Spider mites|Speckled leaves|Rinse undersides and reduce drought stress.||Caterpillars|Chewed leaves or petals|Handpick and remove damaged parts.||Slugs/snails|Ragged holes and slime|Remove hiding spots and check at night.||Powdery mildew|White powder|Improve airflow and sun.||Root rot|Wilting with wet soil|Improve drainage and reduce watering.
