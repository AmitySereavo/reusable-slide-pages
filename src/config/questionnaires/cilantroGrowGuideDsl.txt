# [c2] Quick Rescue
GUIDETABLE|What you see|Most likely cause|What to do first||Plant wilts after planting|Transplant shock, dry root ball, too much sun|Shade 1 to 3 days, water gently, avoid strong fertilizer.||Leaves turn pale or yellow|Low nitrogen, overwatering, poor light, root stress|Check drainage, give brighter light, feed lightly after settling.||Plant sends flower stalk|Bolting from heat, age, dry soil, or stress|Harvest leaves, pinch flower stalk, plant a new batch for leaves.||Flat leaves turn feathery|Plant moving into flowering/seed stage|Harvest remaining leaves or allow seed production.||Sticky new growth|Aphids or whiteflies|Spray undersides with water or mild soap.||Seedlings cut off near soil|Cutworms|Check at night or early morning and remove worms.

# [c2] Care Basics
GUIDETABLE|Care area|Guide||Temperature|Cilantro naturally has a short leaf window in hot weather.||Sun|Use morning sun and afternoon relief in very hot spaces.||Water|Keep evenly moist but not waterlogged.||Feeding|Feed lightly; avoid harsh fertilizer on stressed seedlings.||Bolting|Bolting is not always failure. It means the plant is moving to flowers and seed.||Harvest|Harvest leaves early and sow fresh batches for ongoing leaves.

# [c2] Pests
# [c2] Diseases
GUIDETABLE|Problem|Signs|Action||Aphids|Sticky curled new growth|Spray undersides and repeat every 3 to 5 days.||Whiteflies|Tiny white insects fly up|Spray undersides and manage weeds.||Cutworms|Seedlings cut near soil|Use collars and check soil at night.||Caterpillars|Holes in leaves|Handpick and remove badly damaged leaves.||Powdery mildew|White powdery coating|Improve airflow and avoid crowding.||Leaf spot|Dark wet spots|Remove affected leaves and avoid overhead watering.
