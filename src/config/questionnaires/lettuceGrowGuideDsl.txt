# [c2] Quick Rescue
## [c3] Use this slide first when something looks wrong.
BR
GUIDETABLE|Problem|First action||Seedling wilts after planting|Shade for 2 to 3 days. Water gently. Transplant in the evening or early morning next time.||Leaves have holes|Check early morning or evening for caterpillars, slugs, snails, or grasshoppers. Remove pests and use barriers where needed.||Leaves look sticky or curled|Check underside of leaves for aphids or whiteflies. Spray a mild soap solution and repeat every 3 to 5 days.||Leaf edges turn brown|Give afternoon shade, water evenly, and avoid strong fertilizer.||Tall center stalk appears|This is bolting. Harvest immediately and plant fresh seedlings.

# [c2] Hardening Off
## [c3] Slowly prepare tray seedlings for sun, wind, and outdoor conditions.
BR
GUIDETABLE|Timing|Exposure||Day 1 to 2|Bright shade for 2 to 3 hours.||Day 3 to 4|Morning sun for 2 to 4 hours.||Day 5 to 6|Morning sun plus bright afternoon shade.||Day 7|Ready to transplant, preferably late evening.
BR
[c3] Keep tray cells moist while hardening off. If seedlings wilt badly, move them back to shade and water gently.

# [c2] Transplanting From Seedling Trays
BR
GUIDETABLE|Step|What to do||1|Water the seedling tray 1 to 2 hours before planting.||2|Prepare the holes before removing seedlings from the tray.||3|Gently push each seedling out from the bottom of the tray cell.||4|Keep as much of the root ball together as possible.||5|Plant at the same depth as the tray soil level.||6|Do not bury the center growing point or crown.||7|Water immediately after planting.||8|Give light shade for 2 to 3 days if the sun is hot.
BR
[c2] Lettuce warning:
[c3] Do not cover the middle crown with soil. If soil packs into the center, the plant can rot or grow poorly.

# [c2] Sunlight, Heat, Shade, and Water
BR
GUIDEGRID|Best sun::Morning sun||Hot conditions::4 to 6 hours sun plus afternoon shade||Best shade period::12 p.m. to 3 p.m. if heat is strong||Shade cloth::30 to 50 percent shade in hot conditions||First 3 days::Check morning and evening||First 7 days::Keep soil evenly moist||Established plants::Water when the top 1 inch of soil begins to dry
BR
[c3] Water at soil level where possible. Avoid splashing soil onto leaves and avoid soaking leaves late in the evening.

# [c2] Soil and Feeding Guide
BR
GUIDEGRID|Soil texture::Loose, rich, and well-draining||Organic matter::Compost or well-rotted manure||Drainage::Water should not sit around the roots||Simple container mix::50 percent topsoil or potting mix, 30 percent compost, 20 percent coco coir or drainage-supporting material
BR
GUIDETABLE|Timing|Feed lightly||At transplant|Use compost in the soil.||After 7 to 10 days|Give a light liquid feed or mild nitrogen feed.||During active leaf growth|Feed lightly every 10 to 14 days.
BR
[c3] Avoid overfeeding. Too much fertilizer can cause soft weak leaves, leaf burn, bitter taste, pest problems, and rot in wet weather.

# [c2] Common Lettuce Pests
BR
GUIDETABLE|Pest|Signs|Action||Aphids|Small soft insects, curled leaves, sticky leaves, ants|Spray off with water or mild soap. Repeat every 3 to 5 days.||Whiteflies|Tiny white insects fly up when touched|Spray under leaves, use yellow sticky traps, keep weeds down.||Caterpillars|Holes in leaves, black droppings, chewed edges|Check early morning or evening and handpick.||Slugs and snails|Ragged holes and slime trails, worse after rain|Remove hiding spots and check at night or early morning.
BR
[c3] Spray early morning or late evening, not in harsh sun. Always test homemade spray on a few leaves first.

# [c2] Common Lettuce Diseases
BR
GUIDETABLE|Disease|Signs|Prevention or response||Damping off|Young seedling collapses near soil line|Avoid overwatering, improve airflow, use clean mix, do not overcrowd trays.||Lettuce drop|Lower leaves rot near soil and plant collapses|Improve spacing, remove infected plants, avoid wet leaves sitting on soil.||Downy mildew|Yellow patches above, gray or purple growth underneath|Improve airflow and avoid overhead watering.||Bacterial soft rot|Wet slimy rot, bad smell, collapsed leaves|Remove affected leaves or plants and reduce moisture.
BR
[c3] Disease prevention starts with clean hands, clean tools, good spacing, morning watering, and removing diseased leaves early.

# [c2] Clean-Hands and Tobacco Warning
BR
[c3] Do not smoke, handle cigarettes, or handle tobacco products and then touch seedlings without washing your hands first.
BR
GUIDETABLE|Before handling seedlings|Keep it clean||Hands|Wash hands with soap and water.||Cigarettes|Do not rest cigarettes on seedling trays. Do not blow smoke directly onto plants.||Diseased plants|Do not handle diseased plants and then touch healthy seedlings.||Tools|Clean scissors, knives, and tools before harvest.||Growing area|Remove diseased leaves from the growing area.
BR
[c3] This rule is especially important for small farmers handling many seedlings from trays.

# [c2] Leaf Color, Bolting, and Bitter Lettuce
BR
GUIDETABLE|What you see|Meaning|Response||Bright green, firm leaves|Healthy plant|Continue steady watering.||Pale green leaves|Low nitrogen or poor light|Feed lightly and improve sunlight.||Brown edges|Heat, dry soil, or fertilizer burn|Shade, water evenly, reduce fertilizer strength.||Limp at midday only|Heat stress|Shade during the hottest hours.||Tall center stalk|Bolting|Harvest immediately.
BR
[c3] Bolting means lettuce is switching from leaf production to flowering or seed production. Main causes are heat, plant age, dry soil, and stress.

# [c2] Small Farmer and Home Garden Notes
BR
GUIDETABLE|Grower type|Best approach||Small farmers planting many seedlings|Use raised beds if soil holds water. Keep rows straight. Use 8 to 12 inch spacing for most leaf lettuce. Use 30 to 50 percent shade cloth in hot conditions. Plant batches every 7 to 14 days for continuous harvest.||Home garden customers buying 2 to 6 seedlings|Use a container at least 6 to 8 inches deep with drainage holes. Space plants 8 to 10 inches apart. Give morning sun and afternoon shade. Water before leaves wilt. Harvest outer leaves every 3 to 7 days once plants are large enough.
