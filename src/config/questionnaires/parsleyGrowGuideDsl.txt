# [c2] Quick Rescue
GUIDETABLE|What you see|Most likely cause|What to do first||Wilting after planting|Transplant shock, dry root ball, heat|Shade briefly and water gently.||Yellow leaves|Too wet, low nutrients, old leaves, root stress|Check drainage and remove old leaves.||Slow growth|Low light, weak roots, poor feeding|Give brighter light and feed lightly after recovery.||Holes in leaves|Caterpillars or beetles|Inspect and handpick.||Sticky curled leaves|Aphids or whiteflies|Rinse undersides and monitor.||Leaf spots|Wet leaves and poor airflow|Remove spotted leaves and water at soil level.

# [c2] Care Basics
GUIDETABLE|Care area|Guide||Sun|Give bright light, with afternoon relief during heat.||Water|Keep evenly moist. Parsley suffers when it dries hard.||Soil|Use compost-rich soil that drains well.||Feeding|Feed lightly if harvesting often.||Harvest|Cut outer stems near the base and leave center growth.||Containers|Use deeper containers than shallow trays for better roots.

# [c2] Pests
# [c2] Diseases
GUIDETABLE|Problem|Signs|Action||Aphids|Sticky curled growth|Rinse and repeat checks.||Whiteflies|Tiny white insects under leaves|Spray undersides and manage weeds.||Caterpillars|Chewed leaves|Handpick when damage is high.||Leaf spot|Spotted leaves|Remove affected leaves and improve airflow.||Powdery mildew|White powder|Avoid crowding and wet leaves overnight.||Root rot|Yellowing with wet soil|Improve drainage and reduce watering.
