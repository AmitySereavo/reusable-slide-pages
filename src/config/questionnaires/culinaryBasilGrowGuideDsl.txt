# [c2] Quick Rescue
GUIDETABLE|What you see|Most likely cause|What to do first||Wilting after transplant|Transplant shock, dry root ball, hot sun|Shade briefly, water gently, and avoid strong fertilizer.||Yellow leaves|Too much water, poor drainage, low feeding, or old leaves|Check drainage, remove old leaves, feed lightly after recovery.||Tall plant with flowers|Bolting or not enough pruning|Pinch flower tips and harvest regularly.||Black stems or collapse|Stem/root rot from wet soil|Improve drainage and remove badly affected plants.||Holes in leaves|Caterpillars or beetles|Inspect early morning and remove pests.||Sticky curled growth|Aphids or whiteflies|Spray undersides and repeat every few days.

# [c2] Care Basics
GUIDETABLE|Care area|Guide||Sun|Give strong morning sun and bright light. Avoid long periods of harsh drought stress.||Water|Water deeply, then allow the top layer to begin drying before watering again.||Feeding|Use compost or light balanced feeding if harvesting often.||Pruning|Pinch tips regularly so plants branch instead of flowering early.||Containers|Use pots with drainage holes and enough root space.||Harvest|Harvest leaves often, but do not strip the whole plant at once.

# [c2] Pests
# [c2] Diseases
GUIDETABLE|Problem|Signs|Action||Aphids|Sticky curled shoots|Rinse, then mild soap if needed.||Whiteflies|Tiny white insects under leaves|Spray undersides and use sticky traps.||Caterpillars|Chewed leaves|Handpick and remove damaged leaves.||Leaf spots|Brown or black spots|Remove affected leaves and keep leaves dry overnight.||Downy mildew|Yellowing and gray growth under leaves|Improve airflow, avoid overhead watering, remove affected growth.||Root rot|Yellowing, wilting, wet soil|Reduce watering and improve drainage.
