# [c2] Quick Rescue
GUIDETABLE|What you see|Most likely cause|What to do first||Seedling wilts|Transplant shock, hot sun, dry roots|Shade briefly and water gently.||Plant bolts fast|Heat, age, dry soil, or stress|Harvest leaves and allow some seed heads if wanted.||Yellow leaves|Too wet, hungry soil, poor light|Check drainage and feed lightly after recovery.||Stems fall over|Wind, weak light, or crowding|Give support, thin crowded plants, improve light.||Chewed leaves|Caterpillars or beetles|Inspect early and handpick.||Sticky growth|Aphids|Spray with water and repeat checks.

# [c2] Care Basics
GUIDETABLE|Care area|Guide||Sun|Give bright light. Afternoon relief helps in intense heat.||Water|Keep soil evenly moist, especially while young.||Soil|Use loose soil with compost and good drainage.||Feeding|Feed lightly; too much nitrogen can create weak growth.||Bolting|Dill naturally flowers as it matures, especially in heat.||Harvest|Take small leaf harvests often and leave some plants for seed heads.

# [c2] Pests
# [c2] Diseases
GUIDETABLE|Problem|Signs|Action||Aphids|Clusters on soft growth|Spray with water and monitor.||Caterpillars|Chewed leaves|Handpick when damage is high; some butterfly larvae may use dill.||Leaf spot|Brown spots|Remove affected leaves and improve airflow.||Powdery mildew|White powder|Improve airflow and avoid wet leaves overnight.||Root rot|Yellowing with wet soil|Improve drainage and reduce watering.||Weak growth|Crowding or low light|Thin plants and improve sunlight.
