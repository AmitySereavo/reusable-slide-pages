# [c2] Quick Rescue
GUIDETABLE|What you see|Most likely cause|What to do first||Wilting after planting|Shock, dry roots, hot sun|Shade briefly and water gently.||Yellow leaves|Wet soil, hunger, root stress|Check drainage and feed lightly after recovery.||Leggy growth|Low light or no pruning|Move to brighter light and pinch tips.||Brown leaf edges|Dry stress, heat, fertilizer burn|Water evenly and reduce harsh feeding.||Sticky curled growth|Aphids or whiteflies|Rinse undersides and monitor.||Leaf spots|Wet foliage or poor airflow|Remove affected leaves and improve airflow.

# [c2] Quick Numbers
GUIDEGRID|Common names::Costa Rican peppermint / Jamaican tree mint||Main use::Aromatic leaves for tea and garden use||Sunlight::Bright morning sun or partial sun||Soil::Rich, well-drained soil||Water::Even moisture; avoid waterlogging||Container::Use a pot with strong drainage||Pruning::Pinch and cut back to keep plant bushy||Harvest::Take young healthy leaves and avoid stripping the plant

# [c2] Care Basics
GUIDETABLE|Care area|Guide||Light|Use bright light with protection from punishing afternoon heat.||Water|Keep evenly moist but not swampy.||Soil|Use compost-rich soil that drains freely.||Feeding|Feed lightly after harvests if growth slows.||Pruning|Pinch tips so the plant branches.||Containers|Container growing helps manage size and placement.

# [c2] Pests
# [c2] Diseases
GUIDETABLE|Problem|Signs|Action||Aphids|Sticky soft growth|Rinse and repeat checks.||Whiteflies|Tiny white insects fly up|Spray undersides and reduce weeds.||Mites|Speckled dusty leaves|Rinse undersides and avoid drought stress.||Leaf spot|Brown spots|Remove affected leaves and water at soil level.||Root rot|Yellowing with wet soil|Improve drainage quickly.||Weak growth|Low light or old woody stems|Prune and move to brighter light.
