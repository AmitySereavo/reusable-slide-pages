# [c2] Quick Rescue
GUIDETABLE|What you see|Most likely cause|What to do first||Plant wilts but soil is wet|Root rot or poor drainage|Stop watering, improve drainage, and move pot out of standing water.||Leaves dry after pruning|Too much leaf removal, root stress, or wet roots|Prune lightly and check drainage.||Yellow leaves|Overwatering, poor drainage, low light|Give brighter light and let soil dry more between watering.||Black stems|Stem rot or fungal stress|Remove affected growth and reduce wetness.||Fine webbing or speckles|Spider mites|Rinse undersides and reduce drought stress.||Weak stretched growth|Not enough light|Move to stronger light gradually.

# [c2] Care Basics
GUIDETABLE|Care area|Guide||Light|Give strong light and introduce more sun gradually.||Water|Do not water on a fixed schedule. Check the soil first.||Soil|Use fast-draining soil and never keep the pot sitting in water.||Feeding|Feed lightly. Rosemary does not need heavy feeding.||Pruning|Trim green tips lightly. Avoid cutting deep into old bare wood.||Airflow|Good airflow helps prevent fungal stress.

# [c2] Pests
# [c2] Diseases
GUIDETABLE|Problem|Signs|Action||Root rot|Wilting while soil is wet|Improve drainage and reduce watering.||Powdery mildew|White powder on leaves|Improve airflow and avoid wet foliage.||Spider mites|Speckles and fine webbing|Rinse gently and reduce drought stress.||Aphids|Sticky soft growth|Rinse and monitor.||Scale/mealybugs|Small bumps or cottony patches|Remove manually and isolate if heavy.||Stem dieback|Branches brown from the tip|Check roots, drainage, and pruning stress.
