# [c2] Quick Rescue
GUIDETABLE|What you see|Most likely cause|What to do first||Wilting|Dry roots, transplant shock, hot sun|Water gently and shade briefly.||Yellow leaves with wet soil|Overwatering or poor drainage|Pause watering and improve drainage.||Tall weak stems|Low light or no pruning|Move brighter and cut back.||Brown crispy edges|Heat, drought, fertilizer burn|Water evenly and reduce stress.||Tiny pests under leaves|Aphids, mites, whiteflies|Rinse undersides and repeat checks.||Plant taking over|Mint spreading by roots|Keep in containers or prune edges.

# [c2] Care Basics
GUIDETABLE|Care area|Guide||Light|Use morning sun and bright shade in harsh heat.||Water|Keep soil evenly moist. Mints dislike drying hard.||Soil|Use rich soil with drainage.||Feeding|Feed lightly after heavy harvests.||Containers|Containers help keep mint from spreading everywhere.||Harvest|Cut stems above leaf nodes for new branching.

# [c2] Pests
# [c2] Diseases
GUIDETABLE|Problem|Signs|Action||Aphids|Sticky curled growth|Rinse or use mild soap if needed.||Mites|Fine webbing and speckles|Rinse undersides and reduce dry stress.||Whiteflies|White insects fly up|Spray undersides and use sticky traps.||Leaf spot|Spotted leaves|Remove affected leaves and improve airflow.||Rust-like marks|Orange or brown markings|Remove affected leaves and avoid wet foliage.||Root rot|Wet soil plus yellowing|Improve drainage immediately.
