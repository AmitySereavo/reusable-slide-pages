What changed in v0.2
Every feeding action now carries an operational feed specification: product/type, product form, strength relative to the label,
prepared-solution volume or granular dose requirement, application method, and watering/stress precautions. Rooting treatments are
separated from fertilizer feeds.
Important product-label rule
This document does not invent concentrate mix rates or granular grams where the exact nursery product label has not been supplied.
A recipe can safely specify 1/4, 1/2 or full label strength and the prepared solution volume per plant; Codex should calculate
concentrate only after the actual full-label mixing rate is stored. The product label always overrides this draft.
Multiplier
The multiplier is a nursery planning buffer: the number of seeds, cuttings, suckers, sets or other propagation units to start for each
one mature target plant. It is not a biological guarantee and should later be calibrated from batch survival data.
Day 90 rule
Day 90 is the end of the app recipe window, not necessarily biological maturity. Long-duration plants continue beyond Day 90. If a
scheduled transplant, pot-up or maturity check fails its readiness check, that event may be moved to a later operational date without
rewriting the master biological recipe.

Feeding Action Standard
A feed event must answer all of the following before it is considered automation-ready:
Field Required content
Feed product/type Seedling Booster, Rooting Solution, 20-20-20 Everyday Feed, Macro-Grow Complete, or a locked
crop-specific/granular product.
Form Liquid/prepared solution, soluble powder prepared as liquid, granular, gel/powder rooting product, etc.
Strength 1/8, 1/4, 1/2, 3/4 or full label rate. Strength is concentration, not volume per plant.
Dose per plant Prepared liquid volume per plant OR grams per plant for granular feeds.
Application method Root drench, soil/media drench, foliar spray, top dress/side dress, incorporated, transplant drench, or
cutting-base dip.
Watering relationship Whether medium should already be moist and whether the product should be watered in after application.
Stage/skip rule Postpone if plant is wilted, waterlogged, not rooted/not actively growing, or otherwise fails the stage check.
Prepared-solution volume codes
Code / stage Prepared liquid per plant
CELL / plug 5-10 mL
2-3 in pot 20-30 mL
4 in pot 40-60 mL
6 in pot 80-120 mL
1 gallon container 200-300 mL
These are volumes of already-diluted nutrient solution, not concentrate. For example, if a product is used at 1/4 label strength, first
prepare the solution at 1/4 of the label mixing rate, then apply the listed prepared volume to each plant.
Product roles used in this draft
Product/type Draft role
Rooting Solution Cuttings/transplant establishment where the recipe calls for a rooting treatment. Dose and application must
follow the actual label.
Seedling Booster Early seed-grown seedlings after the recipe reaches the true-leaf/establishment feeding day.
Macro-Grow Complete Post-pot-up and established vegetative growth where a complete liquid feed is desired.
20-20-20 Everyday Feed General maintenance/crop feed later in the cycle; actual product form determines whether it is a liquid
drench or granular top dress.
Crop-specific / granular feed Used where the crop needs a specific side-dress or granular program. Exact grams per plant remain TBD
until the actual label is entered.
Product-label inputs still required before full automation
Store the full-label mixing rate for Seedling Booster, Rooting Solution, 20-20-20 Everyday Feed and Macro-Grow Complete (for
example mL/L or g/L), plus the exact granular product and grams-per-plant/container directions. Until those labels are entered, the
PDF intentionally uses label fractions plus prepared-solution volume rather than fabricated concentrate amounts.

Multiplier policy
Multipliers are conservative starting values. Easy vegetative material may be 1 unit per mature target plant; moderate cuttings may
be 2-3; grape is set at 5 as a larger rooting buffer; many seed crops are 5-10; slow or erratic seed may be 15. Replace these with
observed survival-based multipliers by plant and propagation method as nursery data accumulates.
